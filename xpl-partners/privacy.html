Privacy and Cookie Policy.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information xPL Partners collects through xpl.partners, why we collect it, and the choices you have. xPL Partners is part of EPL Group, based in Miami, Florida, United States.

1. Who is responsible

xPL Partners, part of EPL Group, is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us through eplgroup.co or by booking a call on this site.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

This website has no contact forms and no accounts. When you choose to book a call, you leave this site and enter your details (such as your name, email address and the time you select) on Cal.com, our scheduling provider. We receive those details so that we can hold the call and follow up with you.

Information collected automatically

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, the site that referred you, and whether you clicked a button to book a call. Google Analytics does not store full IP addresses. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and Google receives only anonymous, cookie-free signals that cannot identify you.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as IP address and browser type in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to you and to arrange and hold the call you booked.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and working.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Legal bases (EEA and UK visitors)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for analytics cookies, on our legitimate interests in running and securing the site and responding to enquiries, on steps taken at your request before entering into an agreement, and on legal obligations where they apply.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with providers that help us run the site and our communications, each for its own service:

  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Fonts)
  • Cal.com (call scheduling)
  • Cloudflare (website hosting and security)

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or within EPL Group and to professional advisers where needed to respond to your enquiry.

7. International transfers

xPL Partners is based in the United States, and the providers above process data in the United States and other countries. If you are visiting from the EEA or the UK, your information will be transferred outside your country. Where required, such transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

8. How long we keep information

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Details from a booked call and any related correspondence are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any relationship that follows, and afterwards as required by law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of the EEA and UK may also complain to their local data protection authority. California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion, and will not be treated differently for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us through eplgroup.co.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No website or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children

This site is intended for professional investors and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
