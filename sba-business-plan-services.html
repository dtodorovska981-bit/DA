What a lender-ready plan covers

Included

  1. Funding request and use of fundsHow much you are asking for and what each part pays for. In the sample plan: build-out, furniture and IT, and working capital.
  2. Company overview and operating historyWho you are, ownership and management, and what the business has done so far.
  3. Market and competitor analysisLocal demand, customers and competitors, from sources the lender can verify.
  4. Operations and marketing planThe site, staffing, opening timeline and how customers will be won.
  5. Financial plan in four scenariosMonthly and annual P&L, cash flow and balance sheet in Base, Upside, Downside and Stress cases.
  6. Debt service viewCash flow after debt service and the debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) for each year, so the lender can see how repayment holds up when sales fall short.

Questions before you start

Do you guarantee the loan will be approved?

No. We make your case clear and check that the numbers hold. The lender decides.

Why does the plan include Downside and Stress cases?

Because a lender wants to know the loan is repaid even when sales come in below plan. In the illustrative example on this page, the Downside case is the number to fund.

Which loan programs do you write plans for?

Bank loans and SBA loans. The sample on this page is written for an SBA 7(a) request. Your lender’s own checklist decides what the plan must contain, so send it with your inquiry if you have one.

What do you need from me?

The amount you want to borrow and what it is for, your existing financials if the business is already trading, and your lender’s checklist if you have one. A full input list follows after your inquiry.

Can you work from my existing plan or projections?

Yes. Send what you have with your inquiry and we will tell you what can be kept and what needs rebuilding.

How are delivery times and revisions agreed?

Most decks, plans and models are delivered in 7 days, counted from the day we have your complete inputs. Graphic design and websites follow an agreed timeline. The scope, fee, delivery date and revisions are confirmed in your quote before work starts.

How does confidentiality work?

Everything you share about your finances and internal planning stays confidential, and an NDA is available on request. Work is shown publicly only with the client’s permission, with confidential figures masked.
