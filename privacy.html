Privacy, cookies, payments and refunds

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

DNA Pro Plans ("we", "us") provides business planning, financial modeling, research, presentation, branding and website services. Contact: office@dnaproplans.com.

What we collect

When you submit the inquiry form, we collect the details you enter in it, such as your name, email address, the service you need, your deadline, a description of your project and any files you choose to upload. When you book a call, the booking is handled by Calendly, which collects the details you enter there.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry, prepare a quote and hold a call if you book one.
  • To deliver agreed work and communicate about your project.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Analytics and cookies

This website uses Google Analytics. Google’s tag is loaded on every page, with analytics cookies switched off by default. Until you choose "Accept", no analytics cookies are set or read, and Google receives only limited, cookieless signals that a page was viewed, which are not tied to you as a visitor.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies and records usage data such as pages viewed, buttons clicked, approximate location and device type, and whether an inquiry or booking was completed. We do not send your name, email address, project description or files to Google Analytics, and advertising features are not enabled. If you choose "Reject", cookies stay off.

Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so we do not ask on every page. You can change it at any time: . The same link is in the footer of every page.

The site’s fonts, styles and scripts are served from our own domain.

Embedded services

The inquiry form on the quote page is provided by Pipefy and is shown in a frame on that page only. It loads when you open the page, and Pipefy and its spam-protection provider (hCaptcha) may set cookies that are needed for the form to work. The booking calendar is provided by Calendly and is loaded only when you click "Show available times" or follow a "Book a call" link. Each provider processes the details you enter under its own privacy policy.

Where it is stored

Inquiries are stored in Pipefy and bookings in Calendly. The website is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. Project materials are stored only as long as needed for the engagement and any agreed support period.

Confidentiality

An NDA is available on request. Any public use of your name, project materials or results requires your permission.

Payments

The fee, what it covers and the payment schedule are agreed in writing before work starts. This website does not take payments and we do not collect or store card details. You pay by invoice using the method named in your quote, and any bank or payment provider involved handles your details under its own privacy policy. We keep invoices and payment records for as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Cancellations and refunds

Every plan, deck, model and website is made for one client, so refunds depend on how much of the work has been done.

  • If you cancel before work starts, we refund what you have paid in full.
  • If you cancel after work has started, we refund the part of the fee for work not yet done and send you what has been completed.
  • Once the final files are delivered, the fee is not refundable. The revisions included in your quote still apply, and we will correct any error on our side.

To cancel or ask for a refund, email office@dnaproplans.com.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing office@dnaproplans.com.
