Privacy, payments and refunds

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

DNA Pro Plans ("we", "us") provides business planning, financial modeling, research, presentation, branding and website services. Contact: office@dnaproplans.com.

What we collect

When you submit the brief form, we collect your name, email address, company, the service you are interested in, who the material is for, your deadline and any project details you choose to share. When you book a call, the booking is handled by Calendly, which collects the details you enter there.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry, hold the call and prepare a proposal.
  • To deliver agreed work and communicate about your project.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are processed by our website host, Netlify, and delivered to our email. Bookings are processed by Calendly. Project materials are stored only as long as needed for the engagement and any agreed support period.

Confidentiality

An NDA is available on request. Any public use of your name, project materials or results requires your permission.

Payments

The fee, what it covers and the payment schedule are agreed in writing before work starts. This website does not take payments and we do not collect or store card details. You pay by invoice using the method named in your proposal, and any bank or payment provider involved handles your details under its own privacy policy. We keep invoices and payment records for as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Cancellations and refunds

Every plan, deck, model and website is made for one client, so refunds depend on how much of the work has been done.

  • If you cancel before work starts, we refund what you have paid in full.
  • If you cancel after work has started, we refund the part of the fee for work not yet done and send you what has been completed.
  • Once the final files are delivered, the fee is not refundable. The revisions included in your proposal still apply, and we will correct any error on our side.

To cancel or ask for a refund, email office@dnaproplans.com. We reply within two working days.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing office@dnaproplans.com.
