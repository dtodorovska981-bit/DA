Case study

Haraz Coffee House: a franchise deck that turns a café brand into an investment case.

Client
Haraz Coffee House
Industry
Specialty coffee, franchising
Deliverable
Franchise opportunity deck, 23 slides
Audience
Prospective franchisees and investors
Versions
Light and dark

The challenge

Haraz needed to present a fast-growing café brand to prospective franchisees. The deck had to carry the heritage story, prove the network’s growth and unit performance, and lay out the investment clearly enough for a serious buyer to evaluate in one sitting.

Our approach

We structured the deck in three chapters, Overview, Opportunity and Franchise, so each section answers one question a franchisee asks. Growth, unit sales and location performance became a single data story, and the design uses the brand’s warm gold palette with editorial photography. Delivered in light and dark versions.

From growth to opening day

What the deck had to prove

  • The brand is proven

    Heritage, products and the café experience, told in a few slides.

  • The network is growing

    Openings, pipeline and growth that stays open, in one data story.

  • The investment is clear

    Terms, support and the path from signing to opening day.
