Questions before you start

How many slides is an investor deck?

It depends on the reader. The Custel seed deck is 14 slides with a 6-slide appendix for due diligence. The Haraz franchise deck is 23 slides in three chapters. We agree the structure with you before design starts.

I have a deck already. Do I still need a business plan?

If you are raising money or applying for a loan, usually yes. The deck opens the door and the plan gets the approval: due diligence asks for the model behind the deck.

Can you write the deck, or only design it?

Both. Research, financial modeling, copy and presentation design are developed together, so you do not need finished text.

Can you rework the deck I have?

Yes. Send it with your inquiry and we will tell you what can be kept and what needs rebuilding.

What files do I get?

A PowerPoint export your team can edit, and the Figma source. The exact formats are listed in your quote.

How are delivery times and revisions agreed?

Most decks, plans and models are delivered in 7 days, counted from the day we have your complete inputs. Branding and websites follow an agreed timeline. The scope, fee, delivery date and revisions are confirmed in your quote before work starts.

How does confidentiality work?

Everything you share about your finances and internal planning stays confidential, and an NDA is available on request. Work is shown publicly only with the client’s permission, with confidential figures masked.
