The nine sections of the plan

Included

  1. Executive summaryThe whole plan on one page: opportunity, ask and key numbers.
  2. Market gapsThe unmet needs your business is built to solve.
  3. Company descriptionWho you are, structure, mission, location and team.
  4. Products & servicesWhat you sell, how it works and why it wins.
  5. Industry analysisMarket size, trends and growth from public sources.
  6. Customer analysisTarget segments, how many potential customers there are, and how many you can acquire with specific marketing strategies.
  7. Competitor analysisKey players analysed from public data and financials: revenue, clients, funding and the strategies they use.
  8. Marketing planChannels, pricing and customer acquisition cost (CAC) for each channel.
  9. Financial planProjections and funding need, taken from the model.

Questions before you start

Does the $900 starting price include the financial model?

The plan is always built on a financial plan, so the numbers in the text match a model. Your quote states whether the editable Excel model is included in your price or quoted alongside it. Financial models on their own start from $360.

I already have a pitch deck. Why would I need a plan?

The deck opens the door, the plan gets the approval. Investors ask for the model behind the deck, lenders need projections that prove repayment, and visa programs require a complete plan.

Where does the market data come from?

From public sources the reader can verify: market size, trends and growth, and competitors analysed from public data and financials. Estimates are labelled as estimates.

Can you develop the content, or do I need finished copy?

We develop it. Research, financial modeling, copy and presentation design are done together, so you do not need finished text. You bring what you know about the business and we write and structure the material.

Can you improve an existing plan, model or deck?

Yes. We refine existing plans, models and decks. Send what you have with your inquiry and we will tell you what can be kept and what needs rebuilding.

How are delivery times and revisions agreed?

Most decks, plans and models are delivered in 7 days, counted from the day we have your complete inputs. Graphic design and websites follow an agreed timeline. The scope, fee, delivery date and revisions are confirmed in your quote before work starts.

Do you guarantee funding?

No. We make your case clear and check that the numbers hold. Investors and lenders decide.
