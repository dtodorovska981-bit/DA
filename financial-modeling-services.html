Questions before you start

Why four scenarios instead of one forecast?

Because the reader will ask what happens if things go worse than planned. Four cases, switched with one input, show how revenue, break-even and the funding need move when the market does.

Is the model editable?

Yes. It is an Excel workbook with live formulas. Change an assumption and the monthly and annual statements update.

Which statements and metrics are included?

All three statements, P&L, cash flow and balance sheet, monthly and annual, plus investor metrics: NPV, IRR, payback period, ROI, break-even month, DSCR, gross margin, EBITDA margin, cash runway and peak funding need.

What do you need from me to build it?

Prices and sales assumptions, team and salaries, operating costs, start-up costs and the funding you are looking for. A full input list for your project follows after your inquiry.

Can you fix or extend a model I already have?

Yes. Send it with your inquiry and we will tell you what can be kept and what needs rebuilding.

How are delivery times and revisions agreed?

Most decks, plans and models are delivered in 7 days, counted from the day we have your complete inputs. Graphic design and websites follow an agreed timeline. The scope, fee, delivery date and revisions are confirmed in your quote before work starts.

Do you guarantee funding?

No. We make your case clear and check that the numbers hold. Investors and lenders decide.
